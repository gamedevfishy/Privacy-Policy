Game Dev Fishy generally collects or receives information through your use of our Applications. We may collect personal information from you in the following circumstances:

  • Downloading our Applications or making in-application purchases;
  • Using your mobile device to access and use our Applications;
  • Visiting the Website; and

Personal information is any information about you that is personally identifiable, such as your name, email address, or sign-in information for third-party services (if applicable). The personal information we collect will vary depending on the Applications or services you use and how you access them. In most cases, our Applications can be played without providing any personal information directly to us.

We may also collect and analyze non-personal information to understand how players use our Applications. Such non-personal information may include:

  • General technical information about the device, hardware or software you use, such as device model, operating system version, language, IP address, and advertising or device identifiers;
  • Information about your use of and interaction with our Applications, such as how long you play, the features you use, your game progress, and other game-related statistics;
  • Approximate location information (for example, country or region derived from your IP address); and
  • Crash reports and performance data, used to find and fix problems.

Purchases Through App Stores

Our Applications are distributed through third-party app stores such as Google Play. When you download an Application or make an in-app purchase, the payment is processed by the app store, not by us. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We may receive limited information from the app store, such as a confirmation that a purchase was made, so that we can deliver the purchased item.

Use of Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Develop, deliver, maintain and improve our Applications and Website;
  • Save your game progress and deliver purchased items;
  • Analyze usage, fix bugs and improve performance;
  • Show advertisements, as described in the Advertising section below;
  • Respond to your support requests and communicate with you about our Applications; and

We will retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy or as required or permitted by law.

Advertising

Our Applications may display advertisements served by third-party advertising partners. These partners may use technologies such as advertising identifiers, cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your device and your use of our Applications and other apps, including device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and in-game activity. This information may be used to show you personalized (interest-based) advertising, to measure ad performance, and to prevent ad fraud.

You can limit personalized advertising at any time:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads), then delete or reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization.
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track".

Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), we will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads.
